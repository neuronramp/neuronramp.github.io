Important · Read This First This tool is a screening aid. It is not a substitute for HIPAA Expert Determination, IRB review, or formal de-identification by a qualified privacy officer. Use precautions for the sanitized output

  • This sanitized file is suitable for general analytical exploration where the structural shape of the data matters more than individual records. It is not authorization for any specific downstream use.
  • Do not share the sanitized file outside your organization without explicit IRB or DUA authorization. Sanitization removes flagged identifiers; it does not establish a legal de-identification standard.
  • Do not paste this data into an LLM unless your organization has a Business Associate Agreement with the model provider, or you have confirmed the data falls outside HIPAA scope after this screening.
  • Re-identification risk increases with the number of quasi-identifiers retained. If exact dates, geographic detail, or rare conditions remain in the file, consider further generalization before any external sharing.
  • If your dataset is genuinely small (under 500 records) and contains rare conditions, even sanitized data can pose re-identification risk through linkage attacks. Treat with extra caution.
  • Keep the original source file under access controls appropriate for PHI until the sanitized output has been reviewed by someone with formal privacy training.
