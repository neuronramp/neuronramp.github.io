Mission

Diagnose what is structurally broken. Build what compounds.

Billions of dollars are flowing into neurotechnology. Most of it produces nothing patients can use.

The reasons are not mysterious. They are structural, recurring, and largely undocumented. Quality systems built too late. Regulatory strategy treated as an afterthought. Experienced talent walking out the door because their judgment is overridden by founders who have never read an FDA guidance document. Capital deployed with diligence frameworks that miss the mechanisms most likely to produce failure.

Neuronramp exists to make these mechanisms nameable, transferable, and decision-grade. The infrastructure is open by design. The audience is anyone whose decisions shape the field: founders, investors, program officers, regulators, clinicians, and the experienced professionals the field cannot afford to keep losing.

The diagnostic comes first. The interventions belong to the people doing the work.

Steward

A field-level project, maintained by Sharena Rice, PhD.

Neuronramp is built and maintained by Sharena Rice, a neuroscientist and quality engineer working across medical neurotechnology at the intersection of regulatory strategy, venture evaluation, and field-level structural analysis.

Her cross-layer fluency, spanning bench science, clinical research infrastructure, FDA-aligned quality systems, deal evaluation, and trade publishing, is rare in the field. Neuronramp is the durable, transferable form of that fluency.

The intent is for Neuronramp to outlast any single steward and to host contributions from others doing serious structural work in medical neurotechnology. The license is permissive by design. The work is open because the field needs it to be.
