# Neuronramp

**Practical knowledge infrastructure for medical neurotechnology.**

Neuronramp is an open-access repository of educational tools, structural diagnostics, and interactive resources for people building in medical neurotechnology — and for the people entering the field for the first time.

Most of what you need to know to navigate this field is not written down anywhere accessible. It lives in the heads of people who learned it the hard way, inside companies that do not share it, or buried in regulatory documents that were not written for the people who need them most. Neuronramp exists to change that.

---

## What is here

### Guides
Practical HTML reference guides on the topics that determine whether a medical neurotechnology company survives contact with commercial and regulatory reality.

- Combination product regulation
- Quality strategy for medical devices
- Clinical trials in neurotech
- Intellectual property
- Workforce development
- Reimbursement and market access

### Diagnostics
Interactive tools for assessing structural conditions in medical neurotechnology organizations. Built on the intellectual framework of [MECHANISMS: A Systems Account of Medical Neurotechnology](https://sharenarice.com) by Sharena Rice, PhD.

- MECHANISMS Founder Diagnostic — identify which structural failure patterns are active in your organization

### Educational games
Scenario-based learning tools that put players inside the structural conditions that shape careers and companies in this field.

- The Field: A PhD Career Navigation Game

---

## Who this is for

**Students and early-career people** who want to understand how medical neurotechnology actually works as an industry, not just as a science. The gap between what graduate training produces and what companies need is wide. These resources are designed to close it.

**Founders and executives** at early-stage medical neurotechnology companies who want to identify structural problems before they become expensive. Most companies in this field fail for structural reasons, not technical ones. The diagnostic tools here are built on that premise.

---

## Intellectual foundation

Neuronramp draws on [MECHANISMS: A Systems Account of Medical Neurotechnology](https://sharenarice.com), a 120-page structural canon by Sharena Rice, PhD that names 55 recurring patterns explaining why medical neurotechnology fails at the field level. MECHANISMS is available in full at sharenarice.com and is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

## Contributing

Neuronramp is curated. All contributions are reviewed and approved before merging.

If you have identified an error, want to propose an addition, or are interested in contributing a module, open an issue describing what you have in mind. The bar for inclusion is high: resources must be accurate, specific to medical neurotechnology, and genuinely useful to the people this repository is built for.
